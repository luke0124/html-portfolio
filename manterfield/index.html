At Manterfield Building and Roofing Ltd, every project is backed by three decades of family building & roofing experience. Under the guidance of our owners Craig & Wayne, our dedicated team handles everything from architectural planning and structural calculations to the finishing touches — giving you a seamless, stress-free build without having to coordinate multiple trades.

At Manterfield Building and Roofing, we are a well-established family business who have been proudly serving the local area for over 30 years. Our work covers two core divisions — Building and Roofing — with Craig & Wayne personally overseeing every project from start to finish.

No job is too big or too small. From minor roof repairs to a major structural renovation, our team is fully equipped to deliver the highest standard of workmanship.

Our customers get the reassurance of 30 years of hands-on experience alongside our long-standing Federation of Master Builders membership — meaning our work is independently vetted for quality and compliance. With more than 100 five-star Google reviews, our reputation is proven from decades of reliability, craftsmanship, and happy homeowners.
