Extensions by Manterfield

From our initial site visit, we guide you through every stage—including costs, planning permission, and building regulations. We can work directly from your existing drawings or appoint a trusted architect to bring your ideas to life. (If you are unsure whether your project requires planning permission or sits within a listed building, the official Planning Portal and your local planning office are great starting points—and we are always happy to advise.)

We have decades worth of example builds within our portfolio to give you layout ideas and design inspiration.

We work across Barnsley, Wakefield, Sheffield, Leeds and across South Yorkshire.

Recent extensions work by our team includes projects in Brampton, Darfield, Darton, Grimethorpe, Shafton, Wakefield, Wombwell and Woolley.
