About the project

Due to the existing tiles being aged the only solution was to carry out a re-roof.

The photos depict water ingress, rotting roof laths and perished roof felt.

Customer chose a Brown Colour through tile, a dry ridge system, lead valley and white continuous verge strip as you can see from finished photos.
