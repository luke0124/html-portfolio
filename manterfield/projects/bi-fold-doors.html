About the project

Bi-fold internal doors. This is an open plan property but wanted to separate rooms, without disruption. Solution was a set of 4 oak bi-fold doors fully glazed, this kept the light to a maximum.

The good thing about these doors is they can be fully opened in the warmer months and closed throughout the colder months.

Looks very modern and stylish.
