About the project

This project was a full re-roof programme to a pair of Semi-Detached Properties. Also over a newly constructed side Extension. We removed old and defective Rosemary Roof tiles and replaced with a flat concrete interlocking profile. Customer opted for an Old English red with a charcoal grey dry ridge and verge system. The red and grey colours together were a great contrast.
