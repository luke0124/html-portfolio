About the project

A very unique project and one we are especially proud to have in our portfolio, a Double Storey Extension to a 1960’s 3 bedroom Lockhouse and internal renovations to existing dwelling, creating a modern contemporary home .

There are photos showing the lock as you can see the setting is beautiful.

We also carried out a full re-roof programme to existing dwelling and installed Composite windows and doors with first floor balcony.

This extensive project was completed on time even with additional works added and on budget with a more than delighted customer
