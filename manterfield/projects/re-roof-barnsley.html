About the project

We were asked to provide a quotation for re-roof Programme.

Customer opted for our Company due to our reputation, competitive price and experience.

Also, he neighbour also opted to go for a re-roof at the same time as it is a semi detached property.

This meant both properties made a saving in cost and the tiles looked effective all the same.

Customer opted for red concrete old English red tiles with brown plastic dry verge and ridge systems.
