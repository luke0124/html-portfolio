About the project

This project carried out autumn 2013. Shows a narrow garage with flat roof construction dining room extension to the rear of the garage.

Customers brief was to gain a wider garage to gain added space and combine dining room roof and garage roof into one. As the gas board were to disconnect the supply, we had a strict 3 week window to carry out the works. This image displays progress at the end of week 1. Existing roof removed, existing side garage wall removed and partly re-constructed.

Shows, completion of garage, full re-roof of garage and dining room. Bricks and roof tiles all matching with existing, all complete on time, on budget all ready for the gas board to re-connect
