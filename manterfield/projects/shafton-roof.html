About the project

Photos display a clay 10 x 6 roof with a clay 10 x 6 tile with an approximate age of 70 years old, needing replacement.

As you can see, the roof tiles are slipping onto the hips and into the gutter. The felt below the roof tiles was deteriorating and not offering a secondary barrier.

Photo displays completed roof replacement. Customer opted for a roll interlocking concrete tile and a sand and cement beaded traditional ridge system
