About the project

Rear extension with existing external wall removed to create a large family kitchen area. We fitted 8.5 m length of bi-fold doors and a vaulted roof with velux windows keeping the light to the entire room and on a warm day the doors can be opened to the patio area creating a perfect blend of indoor/outdoor experience.
