Building

We have a long-serving team of apprentice-trained, qualified bricklayers experienced in both traditional and modern methods. From simple boundary walls to complex structural alterations and award-winning extensions, we deliver exceptional quality on every project.

Our building services include:

  • Brickwork & Blockwork: Traditional and modern bricklaying, blockwork and new wall construction.
  • Garden & Boundary Walls: Decorative garden walls, retaining walls, boundary walls and entrance pillars.
  • Foundations & Groundworks: Excavations, concrete foundations, footings and preparation for new structures.
  • Structural Alterations: Load-bearing wall removals, RSJ installations, steelwork and structural modifications.
  • Repointing: Restoring damaged brickwork, replacing mortar and repairing weathered masonry.
  • Lintels & Structural Repairs: Replacement lintels, brickwork support and repairs to structural masonry.
  • Porches & Garages: New porch construction, garage builds and associated brickwork.

  • Commercial Building Work: General building, structural alterations and brickwork for commercial premises.

Extensions

Whether you need an extra bedroom, bathroom or an open-plan kitchen diner, a well-designed extension gives you the space you need without the financial and emotional stress of moving.

From our initial site visit, we guide you through every stage—including costs, planning permission, and building regulations. We can work directly from your existing drawings or appoint a trusted architect to bring your ideas to life. We have decades worth of example builds within our portfolio to give you layout ideas and design inspiration.

03

Roofing

We specialise in all aspects of roofing—from a single slipped tile to complete re-roofs. Ageing or damaged roofing can quietly let water into your property, rotting rafters and roof timbers long before damp patches ever show up on your ceilings.

Unfortunately, with the rise of rogue traders in the roofing industry, a large proportion of our work now involves putting right poor workmanship left behind by cowboy roofers. Whether you need an honest second opinion or a permanent repair done properly the first time, our qualified team is here to protect your home.

Our roofing services include:

  • Full re-roofs
  • Flat roofs
  • Emergency Storm Damage repairs
  • Gutter/fascia repairs
  • Re-pointing and re-bedding ridge and hip tiles
  • Velux windows / dormer windows
  • Leadwork to chimneys, valleys, and bays
  • Structural repairs to damaged roof rafters and purlins
04

Renovations

Transforming your home doesn’t always require extending outwards. We specialise in reconfiguring and modernising existing properties—from knocking through load-bearing walls with structural steelwork to create open-plan kitchen and living spaces, to converting unused garages into valuable extra rooms.

Our renovation work covers:

  • Open-plan kitchen & room knock-throughs: Safely removing load-bearing walls and installing structural steelwork (RSJs) to open up dark, boxed-in rooms.
  • Garage conversions: Turning cold, underused garages into warm, fully insulated bedrooms, home offices, or family rooms.
  • Barn & character renovations: Sensitive, full-scale refurbishments that protect traditional brickwork and stone while modernising the interior.
  • Commercial refurbishments: Upgrading offices, retail units, and workspaces with minimal disruption to your daily operations.
  • Complete property modernisations: Full strip-outs, re-plastering, joinery, plumbing, and electrics managed seamlessly from day one.
05

Loft Conversions

Loft conversions bring our building and roofing expertise together into one seamless project, unlocking valuable living space and adding significant value to your property.

From our initial site visit, we advise on the best layout for your roof structure and guide you through building regulations every step of the way.

Our loft conversion services include:

  • Velux & roof-light conversions: A cost-effective way to convert lofts with existing head height, flooding the space with natural light—ideal for terraced homes.
  • Dormer extensions: Projecting out from the existing roof slope to create full-height floor space, generous natural light, and comfortable stair access.
  • Hip-to-gable conversions: Extending a sloped side roof into a full vertical wall to unlock substantial extra room on semi-detached and detached properties.
  • Attic refurbishments & dormer re-cladding: Modernising dated loft rooms and re-cladding worn dormers with upgraded insulation and weatherproofing.
