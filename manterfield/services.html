Extensions

We at Manterfield Builders can provide you with your dream house extension and give you that extra space you need.

Whether your new extension needs to provide you with an extra bedroom, bathroom, storage, office or Kitchen space we are here to help. The cost of house extensions can be offset by the financial and emotional costs that would have been involved in moving house. In most cases to move properties you will have to pay for stamp duty, removal fees and solicitors fees for buying and selling and also run the gauntlet of the chain breaking down.

But more importantly home is where the heart is and many people do not want to move home, they simply have to due to space constraints. Roofing

We specialise in all aspects of Roofing. From a simple gutter clean through to a full re-roof we do it all.

The roof of your property is one of the main components of a house; if it’s old and tired it could be letting in water and causing untold damage which is very often not spotted until the damage has been caused. One of the problems that can occur if the slates are missing or in a sorry state is damage to your roof timbers including rafters.

We can repair or supply and fit new wooden or plastic fascias, soffits and guttering in white, black, wood effect and various other colours.

  • Roof repairs
  • Gutter repairs
  • Re pointing and re bedding ridge and hip tiles
  • Full re roofs
  • Velux windows
  • Dormer windows new or re clad
  • Leadwork chimneys, valleys and bays
  • Flat roofs
  • Roof ventilation
  • Repair damaged roof rafters and purlins

Our full re roof service includes stripping the existing roof slates/tiles, battens and any felt. We will then use a breathable felt covered by tanalised timber battens to attach the slates/tiles, restoring your roof to its former glory. We can supply and fit double romans, Rosemarys, eternit man made slate, real slate and also source reclaimed materials such as Westmoreland slate or we have even fitted stone.

Loft Conversions

Loft conversions combine both our specialist fields together as an integrated project, and if you need that extra space it is a much more cost effective option than moving home.

We can convert your attic or loft into an amazing new space and the conversion will add value to your home. Whether you want an extra bedroom, bathroom, storage or office space we are here to help. From an initial site visit we can help and advise you every step of the way, and often planning permission is not required.

Terraced properties can have their lofts converted into an extra bedroom or office. Semi detached houses with hipped roofs can either have a dormer window added to the side, which usually helps with the head height for the stairs, or the hipped roof can be turned into a gable end which brings the roof right across creating a lot of extra space.

A dormer window loft conversion can add a lot of useable space by giving you that all important head height and natural light. You can also have Velux windows fitted; these come in all shapes and sizes with centre pivot, top hung or even electric options. We also specialise in attic renovations, so if you already have an existing loft that needs updating or a dormer that needs recladding we can provide you with a quote.
