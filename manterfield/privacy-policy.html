Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how Manterfield Building & Roofing Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses your personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or ask us for a quote or work. We take your privacy seriously and only use your information in ways the law allows.

1. Who we are

We are the "data controller" for the information described in this policy. Our registered office is 259a Pontefract Road, Cudworth, Barnsley, South Yorkshire S72 8AQ (company number 08779779). To ask a question about your information, contact us at manterfield1@btinternet.com or on 01226 781344.

2. What information we collect

  • When you contact us or ask for a quote (by phone, email, the enquiry form on this site, social media, or in person): your name, email address, phone number, the address of the property, and anything you tell us about your project.
  • If you become a customer: the details above plus information we need to do the work and run our business, such as quotations, plans, correspondence, site photographs, invoices, payment records and any insurance or guarantee paperwork.
  • When you visit this website: very little. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this site. Our website host may keep standard server records, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you requested, for security and to keep the site running.

3. The enquiry form on this website

When you fill in the enquiry form and press "Send enquiry", your name, email address, phone number (if you give one), the service you are interested in and your message are emailed to us by the website's hosting server. We do not store them on the website, and we use them only to reply to you and handle your enquiry, as described in this policy. If you would rather not use the form, you can phone or email us instead.

4. How we use your information, and our legal reasons

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange a visit and prepare a quotation. This is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and is in our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • To carry out work and look after you as a customer, including scheduling, invoicing and aftercare. This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep records and meet legal duties, such as tax, accounting and insurance requirements. This is a legal obligation.
  • To run and protect our business, for example keeping our website secure, dealing with complaints or disputes, and keeping evidence of our work. This is in our legitimate interests.
  • To show examples of our work on this website and our social media (see section 7). This is in our legitimate interests in promoting our business, and we will respect any request to remove a photograph.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions about you. We will only send you marketing if you have asked us to.

5. Who we share your information with

We only share your information where we need to, with:

  • subcontractors, tradespeople and suppliers who help us carry out your work;
  • our accountants, insurers and professional advisers;
  • building control, planning authorities and similar bodies, where your project needs them;
  • our IT, email and website hosting providers, who handle information on our behalf; and
  • the police, courts or other authorities, if the law requires it.

6. Cookies and other websites

This website does not set cookies of its own for tracking or advertising. However, some content on the site is provided by Google, which may receive information such as your IP address and browser details when the content loads:

  • the website text is displayed using fonts supplied by Google Fonts; and
  • the Contact page shows a Google Map, which may set cookies and collect information under Google's own privacy policy.

This website also links to other sites, such as Google reviews, Facebook, Instagram and the Federation of Master Builders. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they use your information.

7. Photographs and reviews on this website

We photograph our projects, including work in progress, and show some of these photographs on this website and our social media. We try to avoid showing people's faces, house numbers and vehicle number plates. If you or your property appear in a photograph and you would like it blurred or removed, email us and we will deal with it promptly.

The customer reviews on this website were posted publicly by customers, for example on Google, and are shown with the name that appeared on the review. If you would like your review taken off this website, please contact us.

8. How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Customer records, quotations, invoices and project files: up to six years after the work finishes, so we can meet tax and accounting rules and deal with any claim or guarantee issue.
  • Project photographs: kept for as long as we use them to show our work, unless you ask us to remove them.

We may keep information for longer if the law requires it or we need it to deal with a dispute.

9. Keeping your information safe

We take sensible steps to protect your information, including keeping it in password-protected systems and only letting people who need it see it. No system is completely secure, so please take care about what you send us by email.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, in some circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information, in some circumstances;
  • object to us using your information where we rely on our legitimate interests; and
  • ask for your information in a portable format, in some circumstances.

To use any of these rights, email manterfield1@btinternet.com. We will reply within one month. You will not normally have to pay a fee.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
